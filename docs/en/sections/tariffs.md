# Plans connect prices with access

The panel uses the code; customers see the title and description. A period is the purchase duration, squads determine available locations, and limits cover traffic and devices.

**Where to find it:** `#/tariffs` — Sales.

## Workflow

Set the title, duration, price, limits and squads, then save. Check the bot storefront: the plan must be enabled and visible, with a configured payment method.

## Add-ons and two languages

Each plan has its own device and traffic packages: quantity, project-currency price and optional Stars price. Removing a package hides it from future purchases. Device slots last until the paid term ends; traffic lasts until reset or expiry. Enter English plan copy for the English storefront. If a package is missing, compare the customer’s actual plan with the plan you edited and check website sales and bot-menu settings.

## Verify the result

Changing the project currency does not convert prices. Check every period. Without automatic reset, traffic resets on purchase or manually; daily, weekly and monthly strategies reset automatically.
