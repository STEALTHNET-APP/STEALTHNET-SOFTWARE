# Customer conversations

Bot tickets are linked to the customer record. Replies from the panel are sent to the customer through Telegram.

**Where to find it:** `#/support` — Customers.

## Workflow

1. Open the ticket.
2. review the conversation and subscription status.
3. reply.
4. close when resolved.

## If a reply is not delivered

Check the recipient and bot status. The customer must allow bot messages. Closed tickets retain their conversation; reopening does not create a second account. Team notifications do not forward the full conversation.

## Verify the result

Closing a ticket preserves its history. If a reply is not delivered, check the bot and whether the customer blocked it.
