# Referral rewards

Partners receive a percentage of paid purchases made by referred customers. Their link identifies the referral source.

**Where to find it:** `#/partners` — Sales.

## Workflow

Create a partner or enable referrals in bot settings. Set the reward rate and share the link. Rewards appear after successful payments.

## Reconciling payouts

Compare rewards with successful payments from referred customers. Refunds can adjust rewards. Before paying, compare the available balance and recorded payouts; do not record the same transfer twice.

## Verify the result

Recording a payout tracks a completed transfer; it does not send money. Transfer the agreed amount first, then record it.
